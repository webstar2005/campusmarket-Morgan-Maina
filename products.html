Filtering happens in your browser

Type in the search box at the top, or pick a category, condition or sort order, and the listings below change immediately. Nothing is sent to a server: the page file stays exactly as it sits in the folder, and JavaScript only changes what your browser is currently displaying. Without JavaScript the form still submits and reloads the page.
